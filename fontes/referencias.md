## Teoria dos jogos

1. OSBORNE, M. J.; RUBINSTEIN, A. *A Course in Game Theory*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 1994. (Equilíbrio de Nash e
   melhores respostas: cap. 2; jogos repetidos: cap. 8.)
2. FUDENBERG, D.; TIROLE, J. *Game Theory*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 1991.
3. NASH, J. Equilibrium points in n-person games. *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, v. 36, n. 1,
   p. 48-49, 1950.
4. SHOHAM, Y.; LEYTON-BROWN, K. *Multiagent Systems: Algorithmic, Game-Theoretic, and Logical Foundations*. Cambridge:
   Cambridge University Press, 2008.
5. AXELROD, R. *The Evolution of Cooperation*. New York: Basic Books, 1984. (Interações repetidas e adaptação.)
6. DAWKINS, R.; KREBS, J. R. Arms races between and within species. *Proceedings of the Royal Society of London B*,
   v. 205, p. 489-511, 1979. (Origem do termo "corrida armamentista".)

## Segurança, ameaças e risco

7. MANADHATA, P. K.; WING, J. M. An attack surface metric. *IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering*, v. 37, n. 3,
   p. 371-386, 2011.
8. SHOSTACK, A. *Threat Modeling: Designing for Security*. Indianapolis: Wiley, 2014.
9. ANDERSON, R. *Security Engineering: A Guide to Building Dependable Distributed Systems*. 3. ed. Indianapolis: Wiley,
   2020.
10. ANDERSON, R.; MOORE, T. The economics of information security. *Science*, v. 314, n. 5799, p. 610-613, 2006.
11. NIST. *Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments*. Special Publication 800-30, Rev. 1. Gaithersburg: National Institute
    of Standards and Technology, 2012. (Definições de ameaça, vulnerabilidade, impacto, risco e probabilidade.)
12. OWASP. *OWASP Automated Threats to Web Applications*. Projeto OWASP. Disponível em:
    <https://owasp.org/www-project-automated-threats-to-web-applications/>. (Abuso automatizado de funcionalidades
    legítimas, como criação de contas em massa.)
13. McDERMOTT, J.; FOX, C. Using abuse case models for security requirements analysis. In: *Annual Computer Security
    Applications Conference (ACSAC)*, 15., 1999. Proceedings. IEEE, 1999.
